The browser pane gets one more button, "Flotante", next to its close button. It pops the page out of the pane into a small floating player over the chat, and the pane closes. It also opens by itself when the agent opens a page while no pane is showing it (t3code does the same). The page keeps loading and running the whole time; it is the same browser, only smaller.

A · Con una barrita arriba
A slim strip above the page holds the address, "Volver al panel" and close; drag the strip to move it, drag the top-left corner to resize. It snaps to the nearest corner when you let go and never covers the composer. Simple and always visible, at the cost of 30px.

B · Como t3code: solo la página, controles al pasar el mouse
Only the page, with rounded corners. Hovering it shows a small pill just above its top-right corner: a grip to move it, "Volver al panel", "Ventana aparte" (option C) and close. The pill has to sit outside the page, not over it like in t3code, because Workass's browser is a native view that nothing can be drawn on top of.

C · Ventana aparte, siempre encima
From A or B, "Ventana aparte" moves the page into its own small window that stays on top of every app, like a video's picture-in-picture. You can keep watching the page while you work in your editor; closing it puts the page back in Workass. This is t3code's desktop pop-out.
